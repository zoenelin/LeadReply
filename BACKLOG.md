# LeadReply Backlog

This file is for ideas we like but are not pulling into the current build yet. After each milestone, review this list, choose the strongest ideas, and brainstorm again before building.

## Current Backlog

- ROI card: show how much revenue LeadReply helped recover.
- Lost revenue tracker: show likely value of leads that were not converted.
- Lead source breakdown: compare missed calls, website, manual entries, and referrals.
- Business health score: combine setup completeness, follow-up rate, and response habits.
- Demo data reset button: useful while testing and presenting the product.
- CSV export: download lead and revenue reports.
- Simple owner mode: a quieter daily view with only the most important actions.

## New Brainstorm Ideas

- Lead intake form: a simple website form businesses can link to or embed.
- Missed-call to lead conversion: turn a missed call into a pipeline lead with one click.
- Response speed tracking: show how quickly the business follows up with leads.
- Follow-up sequences: draft a 2-step or 3-step text/email sequence for cold leads.
- Lead quality score: estimate which leads are most likely to convert based on source, value, and activity.
- Team notes: let staff leave internal notes on a lead without changing customer-facing messages.
- Appointment outcome tracking: record booked, showed, no-show, rescheduled, or cancelled.
- Review funnel: track won lead to review requested to review received.
- Website widget concept: a small LeadReply button for "Text us" or "Request a call back."
- Weekly owner email: summarize leads, revenue, follow-ups, and missed opportunities.
- Industry presets: starter templates for automotive, home services, restaurants, beauty, legal, and medical/wellness.
- Import leads: upload a CSV from another CRM or spreadsheet.
- Export for accountant/bookkeeper: cleaner revenue report with date ranges.
- Staff permissions: owner/admin/staff roles for later multi-user accounts.
- Notification center: a single place for overdue follow-ups, new leads, and review prompts.
